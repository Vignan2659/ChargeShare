Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. This policy explains how [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] ("ChargeShare", "we") collects and uses personal data when you use the ChargeShare app and website. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account: mobile number, name, optional email, vehicle type.
  • Location: your device location while the app is open, to show nearby chargers. Hosts share the location of their charger. We do not track you in the background.
  • Host verification: a photo of an ID document and your UPI ID for payouts. ID photos are visible only to our verification team.
  • Bookings and payments: booking times, amounts, refunds. Card/UPI details are handled by our payment partner Razorpay; we never see or store them.
  • Photos of chargers that hosts upload (public in the listing).
  • Device push token to send booking notifications.
  • Reports and reviews you submit.

How we use it

To log you in, show chargers, run bookings and payments, pay hosts, prevent fraud, handle disputes, keep the community safe, and meet legal and tax duties. We share a driver's and host's name and phone number with each other only while a booking is confirmed or in progress.

Who we share it with

Service providers who help us run the app: Razorpay (payments), MSG91 (SMS OTP), Supabase and Render (hosting and storage), Expo/Google/Apple (push notifications), Google Maps (maps). We do not sell your data. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your name, phone, email, push token and ID documents. We keep booking and payment records for up to 8 years where Indian tax and accounting law requires it.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app, delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account), and ask us for a copy or correction of your data, or raise a grievance.
