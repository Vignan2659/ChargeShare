## Full description

Running low on charge? ChargeShare shows you private EV chargers near you: in homes, apartment societies, shops and offices. Book a time slot, pay securely with UPI or card, scan the QR code at the charger, and plug in.

Own a charger? It sits idle most of the day. List it on ChargeShare, set your price and hours, and get paid weekly to your UPI ID.

FOR DRIVERS
• Map of chargers near you, with price, power and connector type
• Filters for scooters, autos and cars
• Book a guaranteed slot, with no waiting at busy public stations
• Pay by UPI or card. Pay per hour or per unit (kWh), with unused units refunded automatically
• Free cancellation up to 1 hour before
• Verified hosts, safety-checked chargers, ratings and reviews

FOR HOSTS
• List your charger in 5 minutes
• You choose the price, hours and who can book (anyone, or only residents of your society)
• Accept each request, or turn on instant booking
• QR check-in: you don't need to be there
• Weekly payouts to your UPI ID

SAFETY FIRST
Every host is ID-verified. Chargers are reviewed before going live. Contact details are shared only for confirmed bookings, and you can report a problem from any booking.

## Review notes (for Apple / Google reviewers)
Login: phone number [DEMO_PHONE without +91], OTP [DEMO_OTP].
The app connects EV drivers with private charger owners. Payments are for a physical service (charging at the host's premises) and use Razorpay, as allowed by App Store guideline 3.1.3(e) / Play payments policy for physical goods and services.
Location is used only while the app is open, to show nearby chargers. The camera is used only to scan the charger QR code and to photograph ID / charger.
